Bring the World to Taiwan's Classrooms

The world is bigger than any classroom — and at My Culture Connect, we believe every student in Taiwan deserves to feel that bigger world inside their school walls. Through our flagship program, School Visits with Dom Jones, MCC is bringing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals to life in classrooms from urban Taipei to rural townships in Changhua and beyond.

Participating schools gain more than a guest speaker — they gain a partner whose voice has reached audiences across CBS, the United Nations Association, and Pho Bolsa TV, and whose mission is simple: every child deserves to know their voice matters on the global stage.

世界比任何一間教室都更大 — 在人師教育協會，我們相信每一位台灣的學生，都值得在自己的校園裡感受到這個世界的廣闊。透過我們的旗艦計畫「School Visits with Dom Jones」，MCC 把聯合國永續發展目標帶進台灣校園，從都會的台北到彰化的偏鄉，逐校點亮孩子的視野。

參與這個計畫的學校得到的不只是一位來賓 — 而是一位曾在 CBS、聯合國協會與 Pho Bolsa 電視台留下聲音的合作夥伴。她的使命很簡單：每一個孩子的聲音，都應該在世界舞台上被聽見。

Meet Dom Jones

Dom Jones is an internationally recognized social advocate, educator, and media personality from Southern California. After nearly two decades championing youth empowerment, fitness, and global citizenship in the United States, she has chosen Taiwan as her home — committing full-time to serving its students, schools, and communities through cultural exchange and global education.

Since December 2025, Dom has partnered with My Culture Connect on a nationwide school assembly tour, bringing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals into classrooms across Taiwan. Her message blends English motivation, empathy, and global citizenship — delivered through stories that students from any background can recognize as their own.

Dom Jones 是來自美國南加州的國際倡議者、教育工作者與媒體人。在美國深耕青年賦能、健康促進與全球公民教育近二十年後，她選擇台灣作為長期定居之地，全心投入服務台灣的學生、學校與社區。

自 2025 年 12 月起，Dom 與人師教育協會展開全台巡迴校園集會，把聯合國永續發展目標帶進每一間教室。她以英語、同理心與全球公民精神為三大主軸，用真實故事讓孩子相信：來自任何角落的聲音，都值得被世界聽見。
